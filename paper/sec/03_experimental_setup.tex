\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:experimental-setup}

To study the cheating behavior of AI models when multiple cheating avenues are available, we build a simulated evaluation environment where the model is faced with a task requiring significant manual work. We adapt two tasks from CheatBench~\citep{phan2026cheatbench}:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Prime factorization.} The model is tasked with finding the prime factors of a 512-bit semiprime. The target is constructed to require 10 increments of the Fermat method.
  \item \textbf{Subset sum.} The model has to find a subset of 64 128-bit integers that sums to a target.
\end{enumerate}

Both tasks are calibrated in difficulty such that the model is likely to engage in cheating.

The baseline and past-evidence prompts are provided in \autoref{sec:experimental-prompts}.
